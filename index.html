Your business doesn't need more from your leaders. It needs more from within your leaders.

Lead Empowered helps you change that.

ABOUT NATHAN

Empowering leaders. Transforming cultures. Amplifying voices.

Nathan R. Mitchell is an Executive Leadership Advisor, Workplace Culture Strategist, Forbes Coaches Council member, author, speaker, and host of Lead Empowered TV.

For more than 25 years, Nathan has worked across corporate retail, higher education, nonprofit, and small-business environments. His experience includes leading store managers at Walgreens, serving as a Dean of Academic Affairs, and spending seven years as an Organizational Leadership Strategist at EPIC, where he helped cultivate a culture recognized multiple times as one of Oklahoma's Best Places to Work.

Through Lead Empowered, Nathan helps small and mid-sized businesses address the leadership challenges behind burnout, disengagement, turnover, poor communication, low accountability, team misalignment, inconsistent execution, and missed business goals.

Using the 5 Voices Empowerment System, executive coaching, and practical leadership frameworks, he develops self-aware, confident leaders who communicate clearly, build trust, create accountability, navigate change, and bring out the best in their people.
